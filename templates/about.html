About RecipeHub

RecipeHub is a modern web portal designed for food lovers to discover, share, and manage their favorite homemade recipes seamlessly.

Our Mission

To create a clean, fast, and user-friendly platform where culinary enthusiasts can share their kitchen creations, explore diverse meal categories, and track nutritional values effortlessly.

Technology Stack

Built using Python Flask, SQLAlchemy Database, Flask-WTF for secure forms, Bootstrap 5 for responsive design, and integrated with external APIs for dynamic nutritional data.
