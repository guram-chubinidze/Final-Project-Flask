Classic Italian Lasagna

Category: Dinner | Author: Nino | Published: 2026-09-02

Description

A rich and comforting classic lasagna layered with rich meat sauce, creamy béchamel, and melted mozzarella cheese.

Ingredients & Instructions

  • Lasagna noodles - 1 box
  • Ground beef - 500g
  • Tomato sauce - 2 cups
  • Mozzarella & Parmesan cheese
  1. Preheat oven to 180°C.
  2. Cook the lasagna noodles according to package instructions.
  3. Brown the ground beef and mix with tomato sauce.
  4. Layer noodles, meat sauce, and cheese in a baking dish. Bake for 30 minutes.
